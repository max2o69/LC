# 🏰 Kingdom Chess: Learn Step-by-Step

An interactive, gamified browser chess teacher designed for **absolute beginners** (from toddlers to adults) to learn chess from scratch!

👉 **Play directly in your browser:** [https://max2o69.github.io/LC/](https://max2o69.github.io/LC/)

## 🚀 How to Play

You don't need any complex installation. You can start the game using either **Node.js**, **Python**, or simply opening the file in your browser:

### Option 1: Using Node.js
```bash
npm start
```
*(or `node server.js`)*

### Option 2: Using Python
```bash
python server.py
```

### Option 3: Direct Browser Launch
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it directly in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox!

## 🌟 What You Will Learn (Level by Level)

The game includes **24 progressive, interactive lessons** divided into 5 chapters:

### ⭐ Chapter 1: Meet the Heroes
* **The Rook Rocket (Levels 1 & 2)**: Driving straight across ranks and files.
* **The Bishop's Magic (Levels 3 & 4)**: Sliding along diagonals and understanding color-bound light/dark bishops.
* **The Superhero Queen (Level 5)**: Combining Rook and Bishop powers.
* **Grandpa King (Level 6)**: Taking 1 baby step in any direction.
* **The Jumping Horse / Knight (Levels 7 & 8)**: Gallop-Gallop-Turn (L-shape) and jumping over stone walls/pieces!
* **The Brave Pawn (Levels 9, 10 & 11)**: Forward march, 2-step starting leap, diagonal high-five captures, and magic promotion into a Queen!

### ⚔️ Chapter 2: Captures & Piece Values
* **Free Snack (Level 12)**: Spotting undefended pieces.
* **Point Values & Smart Trades (Level 13)**: Pawn (1), Knight/Bishop (3), Rook (5), Queen (9).
* **Guarding Friends (Level 14)**: Defending pieces under attack.

### 👑 Chapter 3: King in Danger (Check & Checkmate)
* **Saying "Check!" (Level 15)**: What it means when a piece threatens the King.
* **The C.P.R. Escape System (Levels 16, 17 & 18)**:
  * **C** - Capture the attacker
  * **P** - Protect (block with a piece)
  * **R** - Run away to a safe square
* **Checkmate Victory (Level 19)**: Trapping the King with no escape.
* **The Stalemate Trap (Level 20)**: Understanding why you must check the King to win, and avoiding unintentional ties!

### ✨ Chapter 4: Secret Superpowers
* **Castling (Level 21)**: King leaps 2 squares to safety while the Rook jumps over to tuck him into bed!
* **En Passant (Level 22)**: The ghost diagonal capture when an enemy pawn sprints 2 squares past you.

### 🏆 Chapter 5: Mini-Games & Free Play
* **Pawn Wars Mini-Game (Level 23)**: 4 pawns vs 4 pawns race to promote.
* **Queen + King Mate (Level 24)**: Cornering the King against the board edge.
* **Free Play vs Coach Pawny**: A full match against a friendly AI bot with live coaching hints and explanations!

## 🎨 Features
- **100% Offline Synthesized Audio**: Built-in sound effects (move clicks, capture strikes, victory arpeggios, error bumps) via Web Audio API.
- **Visual Move Hints**: Green pulsating dots for valid steps, red rings for captures, and gold highlights for last moves.
- **Star Progression**: Earn up to 3 stars per level; all progress is saved automatically in your browser's local storage.
- **Lightweight Confetti**: Celebratory particle bursts whenever you solve a level!
- **Heroes Encyclopedia**: Instant popup card guide showing the story, points, and movement rules for every piece.
